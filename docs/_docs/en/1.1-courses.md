This section presents the online courses I teach in computational biology, bioinformatics, and quantitative data analysis. The courses are built around the analytical questions researchers encounter in practice, from genomics and transcriptomics to proteomics, metabolomics, statistical inference, and machine learning. Each course combines the necessary theory with applied analysis, with a focus on sound methodology, reproducibility, and interpretation.
