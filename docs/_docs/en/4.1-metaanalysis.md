This course is to introduce the main principles of integrative meta-analysis and
 applies them to omics data. It covers effect-size estimation, assessment of
  heterogeneity, study-level integration, and interpretation of combined evidence across datasets.
   Practical examples focus on transcriptomics, with attention to the methodological choices that
    affect robustness and biological interpretation.
