Applied end-to-end downstream metabolomic analysis to identify coordinated
metabolic changes linked to colorectal cancer. The workflow included data
preprocessing, PQN normalization, differential metabolite analysis, weighted
network analysis, PCA, and PLS-DA to prioritize discriminative features.
This integrated approach helped identify CRC-related modules, high-influence hub
metabolites, and network patterns that suggest promising biomarker candidates
and mechanistic pathways.
