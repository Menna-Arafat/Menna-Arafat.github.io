CrosstalkX is an R package for inferring transcription factor-pathway and
pathway-pathway interactions from gene expression data.
The framework integrates enrichment-derived gene sets, mutual information-based
association measures, and background gene interaction networks. Statistical
significance is assessed with permutation-based null models, and genes of
interest are prioritized through differential-expression neighborhood
enrichment.
